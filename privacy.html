Privacy Policy — ProGains

ProGains is a workout tracking app that runs on your device. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, and makes no network requests of its own. This policy describes exactly what that means for your data.

Summary

ProGains ("the app", "we") is developed and published by an independent developer. This policy applies to the ProGains iPhone app.

The app has no servers.
ProGains does not operate a backend service. There is no ProGains account, no sign-up, and no login. The app makes no network requests of its own — it does not send your data anywhere we control.
Your workout data lives on your device.
Everything you create in ProGains is stored in a database on your iPhone.
Optional Cloud Sync uses your iCloud, not ours.
If you are a Premium subscriber and turn Cloud Sync on, Apple's CloudKit mirrors your data to the private database in your own iCloud account, so it appears on your other Apple devices. We cannot access that database.
No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
ProGains contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind. The only Apple framework it uses for anything outside the device is StoreKit, for subscriptions.
Apple handles payments.
Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information from you.

ProGains does not operate its own servers and makes no network requests of its own. It has no code that transmits your workout data, your usage of the app, your device identifiers, or any other information to us or to any third party. There is no ProGains account to create, and the app never asks for your name, email address, date of birth, or any other identifying detail.

The app contains a small set of internal event names used during development to understand which Premium features are viewed (for example, "paywall viewed"). These events are compiled to print to the developer console in debug builds only; in the version published on the App Store they do nothing at all, contain no personal data, and are never transmitted anywhere.

What this policy does not cover. ProGains uses two Apple services — the App Store (for subscriptions, via StoreKit) and iCloud/CloudKit (for optional Cloud Sync). Those are Apple's services, operated by Apple under Apple's own privacy practices, and Apple may receive information in connection with them. The sections below describe how ProGains uses each one. For Apple's practices, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Your workout data

Everything you create in ProGains is stored locally on your device, in the app's own database. That includes:

  • Exercises you create or customize — name, muscle group, equipment type, notes, instructions
  • Training programs and their phases and days
  • Workout templates
  • Scheduled training days
  • Workout logs — date, start and end time, duration, session name, overall notes, perceived difficulty
  • Exercise logs within each workout
  • Individual sets — set type (warm-up, working, drop set), reps, weight, RPE, and per-set notes
  • App state that follows you across devices — your active program, whether it's paused, and your default rest-timer durations

This information is created entirely by you. ProGains does not derive it from any other source, and it is not sent to us.

If you are a Premium subscriber and Cloud Sync is enabled, this data is also mirrored to the private CloudKit database in your own iCloud account, as described below. The developer does not operate that database and cannot read what is in it.

Exercise photos

ProGains lets you attach an optional photo to an exercise in your library. When you tap to add one, iOS presents its own system photo picker; you choose a single image, and only that image is handed to the app. ProGains does not receive access to your photo library, and it does not read, browse, or scan your photos.

A photo you choose is resized and compressed, then stored in the app's local database alongside the exercise. If Cloud Sync is enabled, it is mirrored to your private iCloud database along with the rest of your data. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the photo in the app, or deleting the exercise, removes it.

iCloud and CloudKit

Cloud Sync is an optional Premium feature. It is off unless you turn it on, and it is only available while a Premium subscription is active.

When it is enabled, ProGains uses Apple's CloudKit to mirror your workout data into the private database of the iCloud container associated with the app (iCloud.com.ArturoGuaman.ProGains). Concretely, this means:

  • The data is stored under your Apple Account, in your private iCloud database. It counts against your iCloud storage, and it is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.
  • It syncs between Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account with ProGains installed.
  • The developer of ProGains does not have access to your private CloudKit database and cannot read, export, or recover its contents. A private CloudKit database is reachable only through the signed-in Apple Account on the user's own devices. There is no developer console, dashboard, or API that exposes it to us.
  • ProGains uses no CloudKit public or shared database. Nothing you create is visible to other ProGains users.
  • To keep your devices up to date, CloudKit uses Apple's push notification service to signal that changes are waiting. These are silent, system-level messages handled by Apple and iOS; they carry no message content to you and are not used by us for any other purpose.
  • Cloud Sync requires an iPhone signed in to iCloud with a working iCloud account and available storage. If iCloud is unavailable, ProGains keeps saving your data locally and tells you it is local only.
  • Turning Cloud Sync off stops further mirroring. It does not delete your local data, and it does not by itself delete what is already in your iCloud database.

If you never enable Cloud Sync, ProGains does not use iCloud for your workout data at all.

Subscriptions and payments

ProGains Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store and processed by Apple using StoreKit. Apple handles the entire transaction.

ProGains does not receive or store:

  • Your credit card or debit card number
  • Any other payment credentials or billing details
  • Your name, billing address, or Apple Account email

What the app does receive from StoreKit is your subscription entitlement — essentially, whether a subscription to a given ProGains product is currently active, and when it expires. ProGains reads this from Apple to decide whether to unlock Premium features, and re-checks it when the app returns to the foreground and when Apple reports a change (renewal, expiry, cancellation, refund). This entitlement information is not stored on any server of ours, because we do not operate one.

Restoring purchases uses Apple's standard mechanism to re-read your existing entitlements from the App Store. Managing, changing, or cancelling a subscription is done in your Apple Account settings, not in the app. See the Support page for instructions.

Information Apple collects in connection with a purchase is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

ProGains uses local notifications for the rest timer between sets. After you finish onboarding, iOS asks once for permission to send notifications. If you allow it, ProGains schedules a single notification when a rest timer starts, so you are alerted when your rest is up even if the app is in the background. If you leave the app open, the notification is cancelled and you get an in-app alert instead. Skipping or ending a rest timer cancels it too.

These notifications are created and scheduled entirely on your device by iOS. They are not sent from a ProGains server — no such server exists — and they contain no personal information. If you decline the permission, or turn notifications off later in iOS Settings, ProGains still works; you simply won't get the background rest-timer alert.

Separately, if you enable Premium Cloud Sync, Apple's CloudKit uses silent system push notifications to keep your devices in sync, as described under iCloud and CloudKit. Those are handled by Apple and iOS and are never displayed to you.

Tracking and advertising

ProGains does not track you. Specifically, the app contains:

  • No advertising. There are no ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics. There is no analytics SDK of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs at all. ProGains has no third-party package dependencies. The only non-standard framework it links is Apple's own StoreKit, for subscriptions.
  • No App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to ask about — the app does no tracking as Apple defines it.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no identifier for vendors (IDFV). The app does not read, store, or transmit either.
  • No device fingerprinting, no cross-app or cross-website tracking, and no data brokers.

Nothing about you or your training is sold, shared, or used for advertising, because none of it leaves your device or your own iCloud account.

Settings stored on your device

ProGains keeps a few small preferences in the app's standard iOS preference storage on your device: whether you have completed onboarding, whether Cloud Sync is switched on, and the time of the last successful sync. These are device-local, contain no personal information, and are removed when the app is deleted.

ProGains does not use cookies or any web-based tracking technology. The app is not a web app and contains no embedded browser.

Data deletion

Because we hold none of your data, deletion is entirely under your control.

Deleting individual items

You can delete any exercise, program, template, workout log, or photo from within ProGains at any time. If Cloud Sync is on, those deletions sync to your other devices.

Deleting the app

Deleting ProGains from your iPhone removes the app and its locally stored data — the workout database and the app's preferences — from that device.

Deleting the app does not automatically remove data already synced to iCloud. If you used Premium Cloud Sync, a copy of your data remains in the private CloudKit database in your own iCloud account, so that reinstalling the app on the same Apple Account can restore it. Removing that copy is done through iCloud, not through the app.

Removing your iCloud copy

Data stored in your private iCloud database is managed by you through your Apple Account. Depending on your iOS version, ProGains appears in the list of apps using iCloud under Settings › [your name] › iCloud, where you can turn iCloud off for the app and choose to delete its iCloud data. You can also manage or delete iCloud app data under Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage. Apple's own instructions for managing and deleting iCloud data are the authoritative reference: see Apple Support — Manage your iCloud storage.

Because these controls belong to Apple and their exact location changes between iOS versions, we cannot delete your iCloud data for you and cannot guarantee a particular menu path. If you would like help finding the right setting on your device, email us and we will walk you through it.

Children

ProGains is a general-audience fitness app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

Material changes will be reflected here before or at the time the corresponding app update is released. Continued use of ProGains after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
